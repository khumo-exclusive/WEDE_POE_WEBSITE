## Project Overview
- Name: Exclusive Clothing

• Brief History: Exclusive Clothing is a local retail fashion store based in Gauteng, South Africa. Founded in 2024, the business was established with a simple goal: to provide high-quality, stylish, and affordable clothing for men and women in the community. Starting as a small physical store, it has grown through word-of-mouth and excellent customer service, building a loyal base of customers who value both style and quality.

• Mission Statement: To provide our community with high-quality, stylish, and affordable clothing, while delivering exceptional customer service and helping every person discover their unique style.

• Vision Statement: To become the most trusted and loved fashion destination in our region, known for quality, style, and warmth.

• Target Audience: Men and women aged 18–55 seeking quality everyday fashion; local community members and regular in-store customers; potential clients interested in bulk and corporate clothing orders; and shoppers looking for personalised styling assistance.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Goals

• Increase brand visibility and reach beyond the physical store

• Provide customers with easy access to store information, services, and contact details

• Enable customers to submit enquiries and special orders online

• Build brand credibility and trust through a professional digital presence

• Showcase available products and collections to attract new customers

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

• Number of monthly website visitors

• Number of enquiry form submissions

• Number of visitors who view contact and location information

• Time spent on the website per visit

• Mobile vs desktop user ratio

## Current Website Analysis
- • Current Status: No existing website, the business currently operates only through a physical store and social media pages.

- • Strengths: Strong in-store customer relationships; loyal local customer base; active social media presence.

- • Weaknesses: Limited reach, only accessible to people who visit the store physically; no central place to display full product range or store information; customers cannot make enquiries or orders outside trading hours.

- • Areas for Improvement: Establish an online presence to reach wider audiences; provide 24/7 access to store information; create a direct communication channel for enquiries and special orders.

## Proposed website features and functionality
- • Home Page — Brand introduction, welcome message, featured collections table, and key highlights

- • About Page — Business story, mission, and vision

- • Services Page — Full list of retail offerings, personal styling, bulk orders, and special services

- • Enquiries Page — Interactive form for general questions, product availability, special orders, and custom requests

- • Contact Page — Physical address, trading hours, phone number, email address, and location details

- • Consistent Navigation — Menu present on all pages for easy browsing

- • Footer — Brand name, copyright information, and quick links

  ## Design and user experience
  - • Aesthetic: Clean, modern, elegant — reflecting premium fashion.

• Colours: Black, white, gold accents for a timeless look.

• Typography: Clear, readable sans-serif fonts.

• Layout: Simple, uncluttered, mobile-friendly, easy to navigate.

• Wireframes: Low-fidelity wireframes planned for all page layouts.
